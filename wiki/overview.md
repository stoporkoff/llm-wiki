# Overview

LLM Wiki uses a [[concepts/file-first-knowledge-store]] compiled by a repository-scoped
[[concepts/agent-skill-routing]] workflow. Raw sources remain separate from maintained knowledge
pages. [[sources/8d30919d7507-architecture#evidence-001]]

All compiled claims and answers follow [[concepts/evidence-linked-answers]]: they retain exact source
locations, cite supporting evidence, and expose missing evidence instead of inventing an answer.
[[sources/8d30919d7507-architecture#evidence-003]]
