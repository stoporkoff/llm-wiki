# Evidence-linked answers

- Every extracted claim must preserve an exact source location, and answers must cite their
  evidence. [[sources/8d30919d7507-architecture#evidence-003]]
- When the available evidence cannot support an answer, the agent must state that it does not know.
  [[sources/8d30919d7507-architecture#evidence-004]]

## Relationships

- These rules apply to the [[concepts/file-first-knowledge-store]].
- The [[concepts/agent-skill-routing]] workflow is responsible for preserving them.
